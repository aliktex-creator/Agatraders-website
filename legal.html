Privacy Policy

This policy covers the enquiry data you send us through this website and through WhatsApp.

Who Is Responsible

AGA Traders, Jamshed Road, Karachi 74400, Sindh, Pakistan, together with its group companies at Ras Al Khor Industrial Area, Dubai, UAE and Ajman New Industrial Area, Ajman, UAE, is responsible for the data described here. Write to support@agatraders.com with any question about it.

What We Collect

From the enquiry form: your name, email address, the product type and the quantity you ask about, plus anything you write in the message field. From WhatsApp: your phone number and the content of your messages, including any tech pack or artwork you send. We collect nothing else, and we do not buy or append data from other sources.

Why We Hold It

To answer your enquiry, quote, arrange samples and run the order. That is the only use. We do not send marketing to enquiry addresses unless you ask us to, and we never sell or rent your details.

Who Processes It

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, which passes the message on and does not use it for anything else. Email sits with our email provider. WhatsApp messages are carried by WhatsApp under its own terms. Our website host serves the pages. Nobody else receives your enquiry.

How Long We Keep It

Enquiries are kept for twelve months from your last contact with us, then deleted. Records tied to a placed order are kept longer where export, tax or customs rules require it.

Your Rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold on you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email support@agatraders.com and we will act within thirty days. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Where Data Goes

We operate from Pakistan and the UAE, so an enquiry sent from elsewhere is read and stored in those countries. Sending us an enquiry is your agreement to that transfer.

Changes

Cookie Policy

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser so something can be remembered between pages or visits. This site uses analytics cookies only, and only with your consent.

What This Site Sets

Only Google Analytics, and only if you accept. Until you do, nothing is stored: the tag is held back by Google Consent Mode and no identifier is written. Decline and the tag never loads at all. There is no advertising cookie, no pixel and no third-party tracker on this site.

The Three Kinds

Necessary cookies keep a session or a form working and need no consent. Analytics cookies count visits and show which pages buyers read. Advertising cookies let a campaign follow you to other sites. The last two require your consent in the UK and EU.

The Cookies, Named

If you accept, Google Analytics sets _ga, which distinguishes one browser from another and lasts two years, and _ga_DP2HTNJQBE, which keeps the session state and also lasts two years. We hold the resulting data for fourteen months. Your own choice is stored in this browser under aga-consent, which is not a cookie and is never sent to us.

Controlling Cookies

Every browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings, for all sites or for one. Blocking them will not stop this site working. To change the choice you made here, clear this site's stored data in your browser and the banner will appear again. WhatsApp and any third-party site you reach from here set their own cookies under their own policies.
