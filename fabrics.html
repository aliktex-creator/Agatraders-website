FAQs

The five questions buyers ask first. All eighteen FAQs, grouped by topic, are on the FAQs page.

What is your minimum order quantity?

− +

Apparel starts at 100 pieces per style, per colour. Fabric by the roll starts at 500 kg per colour. Trial runs and full programmes are both welcome.

How long does a sample take?

− +

A pre-production sample is ready in 7 to 12 days. Bulk production runs 15 to 30 days after you approve that sample.

Can you match a specific colour?

− +

Yes. Send a Pantone reference or a physical swatch and we match it from lab dip through to bulk, with batch records kept per shade so repeat orders land on the first colour.

Do you make my own designs, or only your styles?

− +

Both. Send a tech pack and we produce it as full OEM from cloth to carton, or pick a style from the readymade collection and we run it under your label.

Can you provide lab test reports?

− +

Yes. Third-party lab test reports are provided for production on request — composition, GSM, shrinkage, colour fastness and any additional test your market requires.

About

Fifteen Years. Two Countries. One Floor Plan.

For more than fifteen years AGAtraders has manufactured and exported apparel from Pakistan to buyers around the world. What began as a Karachi textile trading house now operates as a two-country group with its own offices and factories in Karachi, Pakistan and in Dubai and Ajman, UAE.

We are vertically integrated. We manufacture our own knit and woven fabric and convert it into finished garments ourselves. Knitting, dyeing and finishing, cutting, stitching, printing, embroidery, inspection and export packing all sit inside our own operations. That is why we can hold quality, control cost and commit to a delivery date with confidence.

47 Lines is the readymade knitwear division and the engine of the group, running out of Karachi and Dubai. Kamani Textiles trades and exports fabric out of Ajman. Reliance Garments Workshop runs uniform production in the UAE. One management, one quality standard, four names on the paperwork.
