Hoodie Questions

What GSM is best for hoodies?

Most hoodies sit at 280 GSM in loop-back fleece or French terry. For a heavier, structured winter piece, 3-thread fleece at 350 GSM. We knit both in house.

What is the difference between fleece and French terry?

The face is knitted the same way. Fleece is brushed on the inside, raising a soft pile that holds warmth. French terry leaves the loops unbrushed, so it is lighter, more breathable and keeps its shape better in warm climates.

What is the minimum order for hoodies?

100 pieces per style per colour. A three-colour run is 300 pieces, and the same block can carry all three, which keeps the price closer to a larger order.

How long does a hoodie order take?

A pre-production sample in 7 to 12 days, then 15 to 30 days for bulk after you approve it. Decoration is in house, so it does not extend that.
